# AgriGuard Disease Knowledge Base Review Checklist

This checklist enumerates every disease class recognized by the AgriGuard local MobileNetV2 model.
Each entry must be formally reviewed and audited by a certified agronomist or agricultural extension specialist against university extension guidelines (e.g. ICAR, USDA-NIFA, Cornell AgriTech, UC IPM).

## Standards for Knowledge Verification
1. **Active Ingredients Only**: Ensure strictly active ingredients are cited, never proprietary trademarks.
2. **No Absolute Dosages**: Dosages vary by local formulation, country, and soil types. Advise reading label directions and consulting district agricultural offices.
3. **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)**: Cultural, sanitation, and biological controls must always precede chemical interventions.
4. **Safety & Environmental Guardrails**: Check personal protective equipment (PPE), Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI), pollinator safety, and aquatic buffer zones.
